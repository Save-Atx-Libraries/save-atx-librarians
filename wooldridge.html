You are here: The files · Congratulations, Wooldridge

Last year · 2025–26 · F to B

Congratulations, Wooldridge. Students and staff did the work a letter grade cannot invent.

Congratulations to the students. That F-to-B would not have been possible without the staff, former Principal Juan Cardenas, and the library open all year. A library is not extra. It is part of how a school teaches — the same year the district put librarian jobs on a public cut list, then restored them after people showed up. Adults in that building still moved a letter grade while AISD’s recapture page showed 46.3 cents of each general-fund dollar leaving for the state. See the recapture bill, the frozen allotment, and campus cuts.

Wooldridge · B 2026 TEA rating for the 2025–26 school year — last year. The year before had been an F. Statesman, Aug. 20, 2026 · KVUE 2026 list
Wooten · F Same 2026 ratings, same last year. That is the campus the new Wooldridge principal came from. KVUE 2026 F list · TEA 2025 report card, overall 49
Dec. 9, 2025 Mid-year: Cardenas still finishing 2025–26, already named out for 2026–27. AISD announcement, Dec. 10, 2025

He finished the year knowing he would not be back.

On Dec. 9, 2025, while 2025–26 was still in session, Superintendent Segura appointed Nick Martinez, then principal of Wooten Elementary, as Wooldridge principal for 2026–27. Former Principal Juan Cardenas stayed through the end of last year and led the campus that posted the B. He already knew the next-year job was assigned to someone else.

Wooten’s rating for that same last year is an F. Wooldridge’s is a B. About 83 Barrington students were zoned into Wooldridge for 2026–27 as Barrington closed. ACE restart means last year’s staff reapply; the new principal hires.

Questions those dates raise

  • What does it cost a campus to finish an F-to-B year after December, when the principal already knows he is gone at year’s end?
  • Why bring the Wooten principal to Wooldridge for 2026–27, when Wooten scored an F last year and Wooldridge scored a B?
  • How many of last year’s Wooldridge staff returned, and how many came from Wooten with Martinez?
  • Why add Barrington children to a campus that had just earned a B, then restaff it instead of keeping the people who earned the letter?

That is not how a district honors a campus that just earned a B. The B is what those adults did with a public dollar that recapture had already split: on AISD’s FY2024–25 recapture page, 46.3 cents of each general-fund dollar was the state payment. Librarians who buy paper, books, and printer ink themselves are working in that remainder. The letter grade does not pay them back.

Ratings: Statesman, Aug. 20, 2026 · KVUE 2026 A–F list. Appointment: AISD, Dec. 10, 2025. Barrington → Wooldridge: AISD Barrington letter; Nov. 20, 2025 board (~83 students). Librarian plan: campuses under 400, not on improvement plans, then June 18 amendment. TAP / CIP: Principal Juan Cardenas on the 2025–26 campus papers.
