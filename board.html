How they vote, on these tapes

Almost never by saying aye down the row. The chair asks for hands. If every person seated votes yes, the record is “all those on the dais.” That hides an absence. Names show up when someone abstains, or when the chair has to correct the arithmetic after midnight.

Auto-captions garble the names. “Willie Chu” is Kathryn Whitley Chu. “Kintana” is Quintana. “On the dis” is “on the dais.” The counts below are the chair’s spoken counts, not a guessed roll call.
