Recapture, then the budget “crisis,” in the order they actually happened.

This is not one long page. The list on the left is the site. Start at 02 if you need the court cases. Start at 06 if you need the 2026 shortfall. The quotes around “crisis” are there because the word got used as a verdict. The numbers are still in the record.

Four documented facts

What the left-hand list is walking through.

$715.5M FY2025–26 adopted recapture — local tax dollars AISD must send to the state under Chapter 49. The recapture bill
$181M Projected 2026–27 operating shortfall the board had to drive to zero. That is the year people called a crisis. The 2026 “crisis”
$6,160 Statutory basic allotment, set in 2019. HB 2 added $55. Inflation-adjusted 2025 value would have been about $7,756. The allotment froze
48.4% AISD students classified economically disadvantaged, inside a district the state labels property-wealthy. How Austin was drawn

Recapture and the frozen allotment are state formula. Closures, staffing, bus hubs, and which campuses lose planning time are local choices inside that formula. SOLATX does not speak for AISD or the State of Texas.
