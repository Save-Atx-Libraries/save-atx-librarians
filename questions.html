Questions

A closed decision is easy to root for. These are the ones the nickname skips.

  • If recapture did the equity job it was written for in 1993, what job did it not do, and who is still responsible for that job?
  • Why is Austin ISD’s published total $8.3 billion from 2000–01, with no district figure that starts in 1994, while the law people argue about is the 1993 law?
  • The rainy-day fund is $27.5 billion and over its cap. Recapture did not fill it. Why is the fund unopened in the same year the board cut campus jobs to close $181 million?
  • April 30, the staff summary was protect stipends, librarians, planning time, and cut central office first. June 18, the central-office trade for librarians failed and the stipend cut stayed inside the budget. Which sentence is the district bound by?
  • The budget was 7–1–1. The recording does not say who. Why does a hand vote after midnight not name the no and the abstention?
  • Item 10.6 on May 21 was a financial report. Dozens of people used their minute on it to oppose the cuts. It passed in a pile. What would it take for one of those comments to become its own vote?
  • One full-time librarian per campus survived only as a fund-balance patch. What is the August offset, and which job pays it back?
