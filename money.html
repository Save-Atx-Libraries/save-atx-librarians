One year of the check, next to the hole

The adopted recapture payment is about four times the $181 million hole. A local tax-rate election does not stay dollar for dollar. If the higher rate pushes collections above the entitlement, the extra is recaptured too. Austin ISD has had to ask voters for more than the raise costs, because part of the new tax is calculated as excess.

Recapture is not a surprise line. The district builds it into the budget every year. That does not make the entitlement adequate. A known bill can still be the reason the remaining dollars do not cover the school.

What “we have no money” leaves out

The state account that could be voted toward schools, roads, or teachers is past its own ceiling. Opening it is a Capitol vote that has not happened. Closing Austin’s hole was a Thursday-night vote that did. Both are choices. They are not the same choice, and they are not the same cash.

Adopted figures, not mid-year revisions, unless marked. The district’s year-by-year tables do not always match each other. The $8.3 billion is the district’s word, “approximately.”
