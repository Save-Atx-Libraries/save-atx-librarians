## Prepared questions and Blazier fight (`00:16:15`–`00:32:24`)

**Ricardo A.:** vital community; delay? Segura: they have looked; not enough live-in. Delay would grow deficit, transition, TAP risk. Will not change in spring (Méndez/Martin sixth grade, April 2023). “we take a vote together in November. We have time to plan” (`00:18:20`).

**Langford in Palm portables if the building is vacant?** Would look at putting Langford *in the building* to cut portable-city cost; not committed (`00:19:00`).

**Combine Langford and Palm? Why Pettus not Blazier?** Map: Pettus sits *between* Palm and Langford; merging Palm–Langford would skip Pettus or break a boundary through the park. Langford relatively full; only a small percent of Palm would fit. Blazier is on the southern edge of AISD (down to Creedmoor); cannot consume Blazier’s own site (`00:20:31`–`00:23:11`). Steinport: cluster 5,600 seats vs. 3,900 students. Blazier cannot hold all of Palm. 10-year projections; 2029 on the map (`00:27:08`).

**Staff who lives one block from Blazier** (`00:24:57`): kids on her street → Palm; next blocks → Blazier. Why not Saxon Place / McKinney Heights to Palm? Goodnight Ranch / Slaughter will burst Blazier in five years.

**Parents:** you’re making Palm families *pass Palm* (maybe with Langford inside) to go to Pettus; flood took houses; if boundaries had been fixed years ago they wouldn’t be here; “most of our parents walk” (`00:27:40`–`00:29:59`).

Segura: a couple of streets is dozens, not the hundreds needed; “wonky” little boundaries are how they got into this; will look, will not promise (`00:29:08`–`00:31:37`). Steinport: first regular boundary-review approach; every two or three years going forward (`00:32:01`).

**How does this benefit kids vs. budget?** Teacher load “over 20.” Segura: state max **22**; he will not say last year exceeded it. Then the identity list:

> “We have decided to protect certain things. **We’ve decided to keep librarians in our schools**, right? That’s something that’s above and beyond what the state has. We’ve decided to have **nurses**. That’s something above and beyond what the state requires. We’ve decided to be able to procure our own um uh curriculum. We could have a different curriculum and save $6 to 8 million, but I promise you that curriculum is not going to align with our values.” (`00:37:17`)
