## Lone Star Governance monitoring — GPM 4.1 (`[01:27:23]`–`[02:51:06]`)

After public comment the board is in student-outcomes time. Presentation: scorecard Goal 4 algebra, **GPM 4.1 sixth-grade advanced / accelerated math**.

The GPM: “the percent of sixth grade students identified as economically disadvantaged who enroll in accelerated mathematics and persist to course completion will increase from 21% in June of 2024 to 39% by June of 2029” (`[01:30:52]`–`[01:31:06]`).

Staff (Danielle Perico, STEM; Dr. Maxwell, academics): 2024–25 completed the introductory accelerated course, “an increase from 21%” that “meets the current year targets” (`[01:32:47]`–`[01:32:51]`). Superintendent determination: **on track** for the year. Trustees press the difference between on-track this year and on-track in year five (`[01:32:13]`–`[01:32:15]`).

Facts that come out in questions:

- About 98% passing overall in the accelerated course.
- Eco-dis students overrepresented among the failures.
- Hispanic/Latinx eco-dis 20% → 21% versus a 23% target.
- Emergent bilingual eco-dis declined one point.
- SB 2124: auto-enroll students at or above the 60th percentile on fifth-grade STAAR, with opt-out.
- Sixth grade now at middle school, which expands access.
- Denominator includes any-ever-enrolled plus mobility, so year-end 24% versus in-year snapshots of 25%/30%.
- IB at a campus captions as Manson / Lamar / Mendez is not counted in this coding.

Monitoring report accepted by all on the dais. Gonzalez moves, Kintana seconds (`[02:50:50]`–`[02:51:06]` range in the monitoring-vote stretch). This is acceptance of a monitoring report, not a change to the scorecard itself. Scorecard rewrite is the next night’s vote.

## TEA special-education agreed order (`[02:51:23]`–`~[03:02:16]`)

Joshua Jun, executive director of governance and accountability, with Dr. Cherry Lee. “As part of the TEA agreed order, we committed to provide regular updates to the board and to the community.”

“The order outlined 99 deliverables” in categories: Lone Star Governance; Priority 1, satisfy open corrective action plans; Priority 2, identify and implement effective practices; Priority 3, improve data systems; Priority 4, parent outreach campaign (`[02:51:59]`–`[02:52:17]`).

“Currently, out of the 99 deliverables under the order, we have completed 96 of them” (`[02:52:20]`–`[02:52:25]`). Remaining: LSG quarterly board self-evaluation in October; ongoing monthly read-into-the-record of student-outcomes time and closed-session time; monthly reports to the agency on data systems.

Frontline special-programs management: go-live July after a September 2024 vendor failure. Priority-2 items 47/47. About 700 special-ed teachers at the first learning lab. CFAC first met January 2024; next September 30 at headquarters. Child Find September 3: 30+ families.

This is the 2023 agreed order still live — not a new takeover vote.
