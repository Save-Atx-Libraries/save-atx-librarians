## Opening (`00:00:00`–`00:16:17`)

Principal: this meeting was waited for; staff stand.

Ally: norms, QR comment cards from October 3, five or six common questions, then floor. Vote November 20.

Segura (`00:04:11`): “I’m sorry.” 144-year system not serving historically underserved students. Strong neighborhood schools; misaligned feeders (East, not West); wall-to-wall dual language must follow emerging bilingual families; enrollment down ~2,000 this year on federal immigration; financial risk “right up there with accountability risk, special education delivery risk”; transfer policies drawing students out.

Then the extras the state does not require:

> “there are lots of things that we as a school district pay for that are not required by the state, right? **We pay for librarians, we pay for registered nurses**, right? These are things that we believe in. we procure our own curriculum. We could save seven eight million dollars if we decided to use a state curriculum. But I promise you that wouldn’t align with who we are.” (`00:12:49`)

**Proposal:** Dawson **wholly into Galindo**. Keep the cohort together. Combined utilization “roughly 75%” (`00:21:30`). Dawson has **three UAs**; Galindo **receives the TAP**. State hiring rules (years, TIA, National Board). If chains are not broken, interventions get “harder and harder and more rigid” (`00:14:23`). Sister district in North Texas under takeover (`00:11:22`).

Steinport: existing vs. combined boundary; this one *can* keep community together unlike some splits.
