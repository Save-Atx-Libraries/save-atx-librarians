Primary source tool · local files only

Read the meetings. Do not invent what they said.

This page does not scrape AISD and it does not ship conclusions from videos we have not parsed here. It reads subtitle and transcript files you already have — timestamped .txt, .srt, or .vtt — inside this browser. Nothing is uploaded. Counts are keyword hits, not proof of motive.

What this is for

Board video is the closest public record of how the district explained failure, cuts, and closures. Public comment is the closest record of what campuses said back. The useful work is comparison: a claim on the dais, the same month’s public comment, a later reversal, a number that never appears.

Use it with the questions on Ask why. Example tests:

  • How often is “basic allotment” or “inflation” named versus “vacancy” or “hard choices”?
  • When does “librarian” return after a promise not to cut?
  • Do transportation and ADA appear in the same discussion?
  • Is “campus carrying the brunt” said once, or does staffing still land on schools after that line?

A theme count is not a finding. A timestamp next to a quoted sentence is. If speaker labels in your files are messy, the role filter will mis-tag lines — correct against the video.

File format that parses cleanly

One cue per timestamp, optional speaker in the line:

[00:14:22] Trustee: the campus is carrying the brunt.
[00:14:41] Public: my child lost a librarian.

WebVTT and SRT time arrows also parse. List extra files in meetings/manifest.json if you are serving this folder locally.
