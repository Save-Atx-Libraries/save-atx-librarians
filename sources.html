Wooldridge last year (F → B) and the 2026–27 principal move

  • Statesman editorial, Aug. 20, 2026 — “This year, Wooldridge Elementary jumped from an F to a B rating.”
  • KVUE, 2026 TEA ratings — Wooldridge on the B list; Wooten on the F list.
  • AISD, Dec. 10, 2025 — Nick Martinez (then Wooten principal) appointed to Wooldridge for 2026–27.
  • Community Impact, Dec. 29, 2025 — Wooldridge among ACE restarts; principals hire teachers who meet TAP criteria.
  • AISD Wooldridge TAP / CIP 2025–26 — Principal Juan Cardenas on the campus intervention team during the year that produced the B.
  • TEA 2025 School Report Card, Wooten EL (227901144) — overall 49, F.
  • AISD Barrington consolidation letter — students reassigned to Guerrero-Thompson or Wooldridge by address.
  • Nov. 20, 2025 board (meeting transcript) — about 83 Barrington students assigned to Wooldridge, 279 to Guerrero-Thompson.
  • Wooldridge campus contact — home address 1412 Norseman Terrace, 78758; 2026–27 temporary address 400 Cooper Dr (Barrington), 78753.
  • AISD 2022 bond / Wooldridge FAQs — swing to Barrington during Norseman Terrace modernization; new building anticipated fall 2028.
