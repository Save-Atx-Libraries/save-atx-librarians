Why and the Becker slide (`00:06:21`–`00:18:11`)

Segura: show up and advocate. Strong neighborhood school here; not true districtwide. Five elements: feeder alignment; transfer policy; budget (these changes **will not close the deficit**; 87% people; two and a half years of cuts, now campuses); program alignment (wall-to-wall dual language and Montessori) — “directly impacts Becker”; accountability (B in 22, F in 23, same kids). North Texas sister district. “We are really great at making plans… We’re not that good at implementation” because decisions come too late for families (`00:14:23`). Version control: won’t tell one campus a change and another campus two days later. Redlines coming; every comment cataloged.

**Proposal (`00:15:53`):** zoned Becker students → **Zilker**. Wall-to-wall program → **Sanchez**. Emerging bilingual students have moved; “we need to move to where they are.” Also a TAP requirement for Sanchez. If the program does **not** move, Sanchez TAP still continues; a modernized Sanchez could sit vacant (`00:17:32`). Distance questions later.

He offers to skip remaining slides. Room wants Q&A (`00:18:44`). One minute.

Q&A (selected, in order)

**Seats (`00:19:27`).** Rubric: 509 Becker students next year. Draft: Sanchez or overflow immersion at Jocelyn. Speaker math: Sanchez capacity 532, 317 EB predicted, 106 English-proficient; if half of EP stay, **162** seats. Jocelyn 412 capacity, 54 EB + 193 EP → **262** open. Short 50–60 of 509. Fracture across more than two campuses.

Segura: whole-school move “is often not the case.” Sanchez students can go to Zavala or Allison. Conservatively modeled; if you assume 100% stay, “you are a little over.” Confident it will work (`00:21:38`).

**Anne Salivar**, Pre-K4 and second (`00:23:25`): dispels Sunset Valley rumor of a mass move to Odom. Most live southwest; some already zoned to Odom, touring. Not “entitled.” She will **not** go to Sanchez — 15 minutes on the way to work vs. I-35 and the river. Feasibility, not letter-grade rejection. Transferred from a Title I one-way dual-language class (Gonzalez at Croslin, 30 in Pre-K4/K; first-grade certified teacher left in November; replacement not fluent, child translated). Thanks AISD for the equity of certified bilingual teachers.

Victoria O’Neal: Enroll Austin ranking; zoned-to-Odom families have a seat there; other DL programs by rank (`00:26:45`).

**Feeder vs. Becker split (`00:27:41`).** Speaker: you say feeder disruption is bad, then you break Becker. Math assumes many don’t go to Sanchez. Dual language and community lost. **Please combine with Dawson.** Closing two neighborhood schools next to each other; Zilker DL is tiny; Zilker families would choose Becker–Dawson DL vs. staying at Zilker; solves Zilker overcrowding from new lines.

Segura: came up at the work session; committed to exploring. Dawson EB density still isn’t as high as elsewhere (`00:30:18`). Must still solve Sanchez TAP in a modernized building. Board asked; he will respond with the tradeoff (`00:31:08`).

**Staff (dual language):** 1,100 non-EB families already in the four wall-to-walls were a factor. Proposal some parents want is a **fifth** wall-to-wall in south-central **in addition to** Sanchez, not instead (`00:32:26`).

**Funding (`00:33:08`).** Dual language is “more profitable per student” than Montessori because of state bilingual allotment, even after ~$7,000 extra per certified teacher. Don’t lump programs. Segura: agrees; they don’t budget them as one pile. Montessori has no extra allocation. Single-gender (Gus Garcia, Ann Richards) different. Staffing by language can mean four 14–18 student rooms instead of three (`00:34:54`). Complexity costs.

**Where the EBs are (`00:35:47`).** Parent: south-central zip has as many EBs as Sanchez zip after gentrification/Airbnbs. Heat-map density is higher in a southeast pocket (`00:36:23`). Staffing: **400+** teachers on bilingual exceptions or ESL waivers this year (`00:37:37`). Pipeline from UTRGV / A&M; mentor at Becker then send out (`00:38:08`).

**Why not transport and recruit here five years ago? (`00:38:47`).** Applause. “We’re being penalized for not having enough EBs” when 78704 has more by number than 78702. Staff: new tool is an **unzoned** campus — Enroll Austin can hold **at least 50% EB / up to 50% non-EB**. Couldn’t do that on a zoned campus (`00:39:40`). Parent: why not unzone Becker? Staff: current Sanchez attendees already get transportation from south/west.

**Barrett (`00:41:33`):** nine teachers on waivers here now; recruitment in El Paso, Laredo, San Antonio (9 hours / family). Casey Elementary (his wife principal): dual students there outperformed Becker’s last year — one-strand DL is not automatically worse than wall-to-wall (`00:43:15`). Segura: bigger decisions now because smaller ones weren’t made five years ago. Density maps exist; not shared broadly; trustees have seen them (`00:44:03`).

Field-trip chaperones jump the line (`00:44:36`).

**Oak Hill / Casey expansion (`00:45:10`).** Segura: trustees asked how AISD leans into dual language; 58 campuses have it; expansion over ~18 months; “I know Trustee Singh and Trustee Kaufman are hyperfocused” (`00:48:27`).
