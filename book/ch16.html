Marie, parent: the financial integrity rating “does not assess the district expenditures such as being over budget on the bond, legal fee expenses, or the size and salaries of HQ against what is reasonable. Please address HQ spending, legal fees and lawsuits, and staffing at HQ.”

That is the entire live public record.

FIRST (`00:02:23`–`00:07:46`)

Montgomery: Financial Integrity Rating System of Texas; 21 indicators; data from **July 2023–June 2024** (the 2024–25 FIRST uses last year’s books). Superior achievement every year 2020–21 through 2024–25; this year **95**, highest in five years (`00:03:59`).

The one fail: indicator 4 — timely payments. November 2023 sales-tax payment **one day late**. TRS, TWC, IRS: paid. Has not happened since (`00:04:00`).

Indicator 8 (current assets to current liabilities): 6 → 8. Indicator 13 (administrative cost ratio): 8 → **10**. TEA standard 0.0855; AISD 0.0835 in 2023–24, after years above the line (peak 2022–23). Peer slide: Arlington, Cypress below; Dallas above; Houston on the line (`00:06:33`). Bond ratings (Moody’s, Fitch, Kroll, S&P) at the top; ASBO and GFOA certificates (`00:07:08`).

Trustee questions (`00:07:46`–`00:14:13`)

Hunter: 2023–24 data is the 2025 rating; next year’s books this time next year. Cy-Fair student count — **118,000**; “demogra- demographics are not ours.” Segura: Cypress is not an urban district; Dallas, Houston, Austin are (`00:09:19`–`00:10:17`). Hunter: “When you have a bigger lift, it’s harder… you can look at these numbers on a chart, but understand that the work that’s going on is not these blue boxes. It’s much bigger and it did cost money” (`00:10:27`).

Another trustee: grateful the admin-cost ratio came down after a Dallas-like spike; the number is **two budget cycles ago**; they have kept cutting toward classrooms. Northside “does look more like us”; flatter org chart; “we know that we’re going to have to make some more cuts” (`00:10:54`–`00:12:17`).

Segura: do not copy peers without the story; **coding** matters — instructional coaches coded centrally so they can be moved, which changes how the ratio reads (`00:13:01`).

Boswell: “really glad to see that remaining so strong… financially sound even as we’re really working to remain that way in a really challenging time” (`00:13:41`).
